RuleSpec programs of the Axiom Foundation

The files under this folder (each .yaml program and its .test.yaml tests,
under us/) are copies of files of rulespec-us, the RuleSpec corpus of the
Axiom Foundation:

    https://github.com/TheAxiomFoundation/rulespec-us
    commit f468c8daeb3c0e7184b85be7fb97585475d74a1b (4 October 2026)

They are copied without change, at the paths they have in rulespec-us.

The Logical English programs beside this folder (the .le files, called
twins, and their ledgers) are translations of these files. The translator
in migration/rulespec/ of Logical English 2 wrote them: they are changed
copies of the Axiom Foundation's programs, and each names the Axiom
Foundation in its opening comment. The Axiom Foundation did not make the
translations, and nothing here implies that it endorses them.
