RUML rulemaps and their Logical English twins
=============================================

The originals
-------------

The files `<collection>/<twin>/sources/*.ruml` of this folder are copies,
without changes, of files of the repository

    https://github.com/Law-as-Code-Institute/ruml

at its release 1.0.0 (commit d2ac818f6ed87c2bcf5b69430adf2efa8ba3c6a4):

- `spec/`: 52 examples of the RUML specification, from the folders
  `spec/en/**/*.examples/` of that repository;
- `tests/`: 28 fixtures of the RUML conformance tests, from the folders
  `tests/**/*.fixtures/` of that repository.

Copyright 2026 Rulemapping Group GmbH. Published by Law as Code Institut
GmbH. Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0: that repository's
`LICENSE.md` and `REUSE.toml` put the examples inside the specification, the
schemas and the tests under Apache-2.0, and the text of the specification
under CC BY-SA 4.0. The text of the Apache License is the file `LICENSE` at
the root of this repository.

No text of the RUML specification and no RUML schema is copied here.

What is not the publisher's
---------------------------

Every other file of this folder was written by the RUML translator of
Logical English (`migration/ruml/` of this repository; Copyright Miguel
Calejo, Apache License 2.0), and is a changed form of an original:

- `<twin>.le` is the rulemap translated into Logical English, its twin;
- `<twin>.ledger.md` and `<twin>.ledger.json` record what each part of the
  twin was translated from;
- `sources/text_*.txt` and `sources/text_*.xml` are the texts that the
  rulemap holds (`ruml:text-reference`), written out as plain text and as
  XML.

In a twin under `tests/`, a scenario that has the name of a test states what
that test of RUML asserts (the `.test.yaml` files of the same repository,
Apache-2.0). A scenario with the name `generated_N`, in any twin, is a case
that the translator made. Its expected answers are the answers of an
evaluator that the authors of the translator wrote from the specification.
They are not answers of the publisher of RUML.
