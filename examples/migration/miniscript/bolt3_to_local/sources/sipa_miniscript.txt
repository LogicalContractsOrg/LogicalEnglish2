each as the title of the page's example link followed by the policy that the link loads.

Example policies (the page's demo links: each title, then the policy it loads):
- A single key: pk(key_1)
- One of two keys (equally likely): or(pk(key_1),pk(key_2))
- One of two keys (one likely, one unlikely): or(99@pk(key_likely),pk(key_unlikely))
- A user and a 2FA service need to sign off, but after 90 days the user alone is enough: and(pk(key_user),or(99@pk(key_service),older(12960)))
- A 3-of-3 that turns into a 2-of-3 after 90 days: thresh(3,pk(key_1),pk(key_2),pk(key_3),older(12960))
- The BOLT #3 to_local policy: or(pk(key_revocation),and(pk(key_local),older(1008)))
- The BOLT #3 offered HTLC policy: or(pk(key_revocation),and(pk(key_remote),or(pk(key_local),hash160(H))))
- The BOLT #3 received HTLC policy: or(pk(key_revocation),and(pk(key_remote),or(and(pk(key_local),hash160(H)),older(1008))))
