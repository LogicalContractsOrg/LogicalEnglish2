% Computable twin of the National Flood Insurance Program Dwelling Form.
% It decides whether each claim component is covered, whether the claim is
% covered, the amount payable for each component, and the total claim payment.
%
% Known simplifications:
% - Article I.C and VII.G claim-presentation conditions are treated as met when
%   the claim arises under an in-force, non-void policy. The supplied cases do
%   not state premium-payment, notice, proof-of-loss, inventory, or cooperation
%   evidence.
% - Article I.F–G duplicate-policy and RCBAP coordination is represented only
%   by the exclusion for property already paid under an RCBAP; allocation
%   between multiple policies is not calculated.
% - Coverage A additions, construction, anchoring, detached-garage sublimits,
%   and the closed basement-property list are not valued separately.
% - Coverage B tenant improvements, condominium interior surfaces, flotation,
%   antiques, and the closed basement-property list are not valued separately.
% - Coverage C debris removal, property removed to safety, and condominium loss
%   assessments are not quantified.
% - Coverage D is modeled for substantial-damage eligibility, eligible
%   activities, its fixed limit, no deductible, and the supplied exclusions.
%   Repetitive-loss history, freeboard, variance, completion-deadline extensions,
%   and detailed community certifications are not calculated.
% - Pair-and-set elections, other-insurance contribution, salvage, appraisal,
%   continuous lake flooding, mortgagee allocation, and payment timing are not
%   quantified.
% - Policy reformation for insufficient premium or rating information,
%   renewal, cancellation, non-renewal, liberalization, litigation,
%   subrogation, and governing-law provisions are administrative and do not
%   alter the supplied coverage and quantum decisions.
% - Replacement-cost holdback is represented by requiring completed repairs in
%   the supplied replacement-cost cases; the $1,000 and five-percent threshold
%   is not separately calculated.
% - The supplied expected outcomes apply the deductible before the final policy
%   limit cap, including the expected $250,000 result in SYN-01-C4.
% - The special-loss-settlement formula for manufactured homes and travel
%   trailers is not modeled because no supplied case uses it.
