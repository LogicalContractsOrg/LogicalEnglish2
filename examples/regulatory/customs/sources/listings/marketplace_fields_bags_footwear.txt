# The fields of a marketplace listing of bags, cases, leather goods and footwear: a description of the kind of text that the customs background reads
# Written on 21 November 2026 from 66 listings of the HSCodeComp benchmark (lpsPlus benchmarks/hscodecomp/blind/blindH*.json), without their expected codes
# The counts are the number of listings, of 66, that have the field

A listing offers one finished product for sale. It has a title, the categories of the shop, a price, and attributes. Each attribute is a field with a name and a value, written as "Name: Value".

The shop's categories are, for example, "Shoes > Women's Shoes > Women's Boots", "Shoes > Women's Shoes > Pumps", "Shoes > Women's Shoes > Women's Slippers", "Shoes > Men's Shoes > Casual Sneakers", "Sports Shoes,Clothing&Accessories > Sneakers > Running Shoes", "Sports Shoes,Clothing&Accessories > Dance > Dance Shoes", "Luggage & Bags > Women's Handbags > Shoulder Bags", "Luggage & Bags > Backpacks > Backpacks", "Home & Garden > Home Storage & Organization > Storage Bags".

The fields that describe what the product is:
Item Type (34 listings): the kind of product in the seller's words, for example "Item Type: BOOTS", "Item Type: sandals", "Item Type: Pumps", "Item Type: flats", "Item Type: Casual Shoes", "Item Type: slippers".
Type (6 listings): for example "Type: Storage Bags", "Type: Leg Warmers", "Type: Collars,Dogs", "Type: Protective Shell/Skin".
Shoes Type, Boot Type, Handbags Type, Types of bags, Shape: the kind of shoe, boot or bag, for example "Boot Type: Rainboots", "Boot Type: Motorcycle boots", "Shoes Type: Sports shoes", "Handbags Type: Shoulder Bags", "Types of bags: Shoulder & Crossbody Bags", "Shape: Casual Tote", "Shape: Clutch".
Upper coverage (27 listings): "Upper coverage: Not above ankle", "Upper coverage: Above the ankle but not to the knee", "Upper coverage: Above the knee".
Boot Height (11 listings): for example "Boot Height: ANKLE", "Boot Height: Knee-High", "Boot Height: Over-the-Knee".
Upper fixing method (33 listings): how the upper is fixed to the sole, for example "Upper fixing method: sticking", "Upper fixing method: Injection molding", "Upper fixing method: stitching", "Upper fixing method: straps across the instep and around the big toe".
Whether with metal toe cap (19 listings): "Whether with metal toe cap: Yes" or "No".
Whether waterproof (23 listings): "Whether waterproof: Yes" or "No".
Closure Type (48 listings): for example "Closure Type: Zipper", "Closure Type: Slip-On", "Closure Type: Lace-up", "Closure Type: Buckle Strap".
Exterior, Interior, Number of Handles/Straps, Hardness: the parts of a bag, for example "Interior: Interior Slot Pocket", "Hardness: SOFT".
Department Name (37 listings) and Gender (53 listings): for example "Department Name: Adult", "Department Name: Children", "Gender: Women".

The fields that describe what the product is made of:
Upper Material (37 listings): the outer surface of the upper, for example "Upper Material: PU", "Upper Material: Genuine Leather", "Upper Material: Air Mesh", "Upper Material: PVC", "Upper Material: Flock", "Upper Material: Velvet".
Outsole Material (36 listings): for example "Outsole Material: Rubber", "Outsole Material: EVA", "Outsole Material: TPU", "Outsole Material: TPR", "Outsole Material: PVC".
Shaft Material (10 listings): the material of the shaft of a boot, for example "Shaft Material: PU", "Shaft Material: Genuine Leather".
Lining Material (43 listings) and Insole Material (31 listings): for example "Lining Material: PU", "Lining Material: Cotton", "Insole Material: EVA", "Insole Material: Sheepskin".
Main Material (13 listings) and Material (19 listings): the material of a bag or another product, for example "Main Material: PU", "Main Material: POLYESTER", "Main Material: Nylon", "Main Material: Genuine Leather", "Main Material: CANVAS", "Material: Microfiber", "Material: Silicone".

The other fields describe the look and the offer (Pattern Type, Style, Fit, Season, Fashion Element, Heel Type, Heel Height, Occasion, With Platforms, Platform Height, Toe Shape, Decoration, Feature, Function, Shoe Width, Applicable Place, each pack). They say little about what the product is made of.
