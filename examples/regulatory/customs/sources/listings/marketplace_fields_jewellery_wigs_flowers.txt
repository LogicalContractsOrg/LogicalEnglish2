# The fields of a marketplace listing of jewellery, beads, wigs, hair and artificial flowers: a description of the kind of text that the customs background reads
# Written on 21 November 2026 from 124 listings of the HSCodeComp benchmark (lpsPlus benchmarks/hscodecomp/blind/blindG*.json), without their expected codes
# The counts are the number of listings, of 124, that have the field

A listing offers one finished product for sale. It has a title, the categories of the shop, a price, and attributes. Each attribute is a field with a name and a value, written as "Name: Value".

The shop's categories are, for example, "Jewelry & Accessories > Fashion Jewelry > Bracelets", "Jewelry & Accessories > Fine Jewelry > Earrings", "Jewelry & Accessories > Jewelry Making > Jewelry Findings & Components", "Home & Garden > Festive & Party Supplies > Artificial Decorations", "Hair Extensions & Wigs > Human Wigs( For Black) > Lace Closure & Frontal Wigs", "Human Hair (For Black) > Human Hair Weave".

The fields that describe what the product is:
Item Type (72 listings): the kind of product in the seller's words, for example "Item Type: beads", "Item Type: BRACELETS", "Item Type: Rings", "Item Type: NECKLACES", "Item Type: jewelry findings", "Item Type: CHARMS".
Type (12 listings): a kind of product, for example "Type: ROSE", "Type: Artificial flower", "Type: cartoon badge".
Fine or Fashion (67 listings): "Fine or Fashion: Fine" or "Fine or Fashion: Fashion".
Bracelets Type, Earring Type, Rings Type, Chain Type, Lace Wig Type: the kind of bracelet, earring, ring, chain or wig, for example "Bracelets Type: Bangles", "Earring Type: Stud Earrings", "Lace Wig Type: Lace Front Wigs".
Purpose (10 listings): for example "Purpose: Home Decoration".

The fields that describe what the product is made of:
Material (87 listings): the materials, for example "Material: Metal", "Material: Plastic", "Material: Silk", "Material: cubic zirconia", "Material: Human Hair", "Material: Pearl".
Metals Type (69 listings): the metal, for example "Metals Type: Zinc Alloy", "Metals Type: Stainless Steel", "Metals Type: Silver", "Metals Type: Copper", "Metals Type: Brass", "Metals Type: None".
Metal Stamp (8 listings): the hallmark, for example "Metal Stamp: 925,Sterling", "Metal Stamp: 18K".
Main Stone (18 listings) and Side Stone (10 listings): the stones, for example "Main Stone: Zircon", "Main Stone: Moissanite", "Main Stone: Moon Stone", "Side Stone: Diamond".
Certificate (17 listings) and Certificate Type (13 listings): whether a laboratory certificate comes with the product, for example "Certificate Type: GDTC", "Certificate Type: IGI,Third Party Appraisal".
Human Hair Type (24 listings), Hair Grade (17 listings), Material Grade (8 listings): for example "Human Hair Type: Brazilian Hair", "Hair Grade: Remy Hair".
Base Material (18 listings): the base of a wig, for example "Base Material: Swiss Lace".

The other fields describe the look and the offer (Gender, Shape\pattern, Occasion, Color, Setting Type, Texture, Wigs Length, Cap Size, Lace Color, Item Weight, Size, Quantity, each pack, Style). They say little about what the product is made of.
