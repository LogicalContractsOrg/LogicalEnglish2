# The fields of a marketplace listing: a description of the kind of text that the customs background reads
# Written on 20 November 2026 from the 113 listings of chapters 39, 61 and 62 of the HSCodeComp benchmark (lpsPlus benchmarks/hscodecomp/blind), without their expected codes
# The counts are the number of listings, of 113, that have the field

A listing offers one finished product for sale. It has a title, the categories of the shop, a price, and attributes. Each attribute is a field with a name and a value, written as "Name: Value".

The fields that describe what the product is:
Item Type (64 listings): the kind of product in the seller's words, for example "Item Type: tops", "Item Type: JEANS", "Item Type: Prom Dresses".
Tops Type (13 listings): the kind of top, for example "Tops Type: TEES", "Tops Type: TANK TOPS", "Tops Type: CAMIS".
Type (21 listings): a kind of product, for example "Type: Half-Wrapped Case".

The fields that describe what the product is made of:
Material (100 listings): the materials, separated by commas, without their shares, for example "Material: POLYESTER", "Material: Cotton", "Material: POLYESTER,Spandex", "Material: Plastic", "Material: Acrylic".
Material Composition (9 listings): the materials, sometimes with their shares.
Fabric Type (38 listings): the name of the fabric, for example "Fabric Type: Broadcloth", "Fabric Type: Tulle".

The fields that describe how a garment is made and for whom:
Craft of Weaving (54 listings): how the fabric is made, with the values "Craft of Weaving: Knit", "Craft of Weaving: Tat", "Craft of Weaving: Non-Woven", "Craft of Weaving: Other", and once "Craft of Weaving: Not Clothing or Textiles", the seller's statement that the product is neither clothing nor a textile.
Gender (52 listings): for whom the product is made, for example "Gender: Women", "Gender: Men", "Gender: Unisex", "Gender: girls".
Sleeve Length(cm) (26 listings), Sleeve Style (20 listings), Collar (18 listings), Neckline (8 listings), Decoration (33 listings), Closure Type (24 listings), Fit Type (20 listings), Thickness (13 listings).

The other fields describe the shop and the offer (Brand Name, Origin, Model Number, Style, Season, Release Date, each pack, Package size, Package weight). They say nothing about what the product is.
