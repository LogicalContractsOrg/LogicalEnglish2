# The fields of a marketplace listing of any other product: a description of the kind of text that the customs background reads
# Written on 23 November 2026 from 329 listings of the HSCodeComp benchmark (lpsPlus benchmarks/hscodecomp/blind/blindI_all.json), without their expected codes. These are the listings whose goods are not in the chapters that the model had before (39, 42, 61, 62, 64, 67 and 71).
# The counts are the number of listings, of 329, that have the field

A listing offers one finished product for sale. It has a title, the categories of the shop, a price, and attributes. Each attribute is a field with a name and a value, written as "Name: Value".

The shop's first category is, for example: "Home & Garden" (46 listings), "Tools" (36), "Toys & Hobbies" (31), "Consumer Electronics" (28), "Jewelry & Accessories" (22), "Beauty & Health" (22), "Automobiles, Parts & Accessories" (19), "Watches" (19), "Sports & Entertainment" (14), "Computer & Office" (11), "Home Appliances" (11), "Home Improvement" (11), "Furniture" (10), "Electronic Components & Supplies" (8), "Lights & Lighting" (8), "Industrial & Business" (7), "Apparel Accessories" (6), "Office & School Supplies" (6).

Some categories in full: "Watches > Watches Accessories", "Tools > Tool Parts", "Tools > Tool Sets", "Tools > Measurement & Analysis Instruments", "Home & Garden > Arts,Crafts & Sewing", "Home & Garden > Festive & Party Supplies", "Home & Garden > Home Decor", "Home & Garden > Home Textile", "Home & Garden > Kitchen,Dining & Bar", "Home Improvement > Electrical Equipment & Supplies", "Electronic Components & Supplies > Active Components", "Consumer Electronics > Games & Accessories", "Consumer Electronics > Portable Audio & Video", "Toys & Hobbies > Action & Toy Figures", "Toys & Hobbies > Remote Control Toys", "Toys & Hobbies > Stuffed Animals & Plush", "Toys & Hobbies > Dolls & Accessories", "Sports & Entertainment > Fishing", "Sports & Entertainment > Cycling", "Furniture > Outdoor Furniture", "Beauty & Health > Nail Art & Tools", "Office & School Supplies > Books".

The fields that describe what the product is:
Item Type (99 listings): the kind of product in the seller's words, for example "Item Type: Watchbands", "Item Type: Watch Cases", "Item Type: Model", "Item Type: Key chains", "Item Type: Repair Tools & Kits", "Item Type: Doll Accessories", "Item Type: Stage Lighting Effect", "Item Type: EYEWEAR", "Item Type: SHOELACES".
Type (124 listings): for example "Type: Combination", "Type: Other", "Type: Voltage Regulator", "Type: Hand Tool Parts", "Type: Watches for Parts", "Type: Car", "Type: Folding Blade Knife", "Type: Canvas Printings".
Category (16 listings) and Product Type (10 listings): for example "Category: Handheld Game Players", "Category: Speakers", "Category: Smart Watches", "Category: LURE", "Category: stickers", "Product Type: Lace", "Product Type: Patches".
Use (39 listings) and Application (27 listings): for example "Use: Home DIY", "Use: Vehicles & Remote Control Toys", "Use: Daily Lighting", "Application: Wood Working Tool", "Application: Camera".
Recommend Age (33 listings): the age the seller recommends, for example "Recommend Age: 14+y", "Recommend Age: 3-6Y,6-12Y", "Recommend Age: 18+".
Theme (20 listings), Occasion (20 listings): for example "Theme: Movie & TV", "Occasion: Christmas", "Occasion: party".
Fields of a powered product: Power Source (14 listings: "AC", "DC", "Battery", "Electric"), Voltage (23), Is Electric (12: "No Battery", "Lithium Battery", "Button Battery"), Electronic (12: "Yes" or "No"), Is Smart Device (11), Power (W) (7), Certification (88: "CE", "CE,FCC,RoHS", "None").
Features, Feature, Function, Special Features: for example "Features: Diecast", "Features: Stuffed & Plush", "Function: Alarm Clock,...,Chronograph".
Fine or Fashion (12 listings): "Fine" or "Fashion", for jewellery.
Gender (43 listings), Size, Dimensions, Shape, Color, Pattern Type: the look and the size.

The fields that describe what the product is made of:
Material (153 listings): for example "Material: Metal", "Material: Plastic", "Material: Glass", "Material: ABS", "Material: Stainless Steel", "Material: PVC", "Material: 100% Polyester", "Material: Cloth", "Material: Nylon", "Material: Wood", "Material: CANVAS", "Material: 100% Cotton".
Metals Type (14 listings): for example "Metals Type: Zinc Alloy", "Metals Type: Stainless Steel", "Metals Type: Copper".
Technics (19 listings): how a textile product is made, for example "Technics: Woven", "Technics: Knitted", "Technics: Embroidered", "Technics: Spray Painting".

The other fields describe the offer (each pack, Quantity, Condition, Warranty, is_customized, Warning, Language). They say little about what the product is.
