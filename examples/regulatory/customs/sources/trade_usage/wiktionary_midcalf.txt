== English ==


=== Alternative forms ===
mid-calf


=== Etymology ===
From mid- + calf.


=== Adjective ===
midcalf (not comparable)

Extending to the middle of the calf of the leg.
Synonym: (hemline) midi


=== Adverb ===
midcalf (not comparable)

At or to the middle of the calf.


=== Noun ===
midcalf (plural midcalves)

The middle of the calf.
