As requested, the samples will be returned to you.Sample J294-5603 (Style 34866678T) is a bracelet that is made of Sterling Silver, and semi-precious stones of Cubic Zirconia (CZ) and Marcasite. The earrings are designed in an oval shape with leverback findings.  It features 310 round Marcasite stones.  Cost data indicates $29.30.You specifically ask whether the samples under consideration would be classifiable under Subheading 7113.11.5080 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS) or under Subheading 7116.20.0580, HTSUS.  Heading 7113, HTSUS, covers articles of jewelry and parts thereof of precious metal or metal clad with precious metal, and heading 7116, HTSUS, in pertinent part, covers articles of precious and semi-precious stones.  Legal Note 2 (b) to Chapter 71, HTSUS, provides that Heading 7116 does not cover articles containing precious metal or metal clad with precious metal (other than as minor constituents).  Upon review of  the bracelets and earrings, we find that the jewelry pieces made in part of Sterling Silver, a precious metal, not to be of minor constituents; accordingly the jewelry pieces are classified in Heading 7113, HTSUS – the provision for articles of jewelry of precious metal.        The applicable subheading for the bracelets will be 7113.11.5080, HTSUS, which provides for "Articles of jewelry and parts thereof, of precious metal or of metal clad with precious metal: Of precious metal whether or not plated or clad with precious metal: Of silver, whether or not plated or clad with precious metal: Other: Other; Other."  The rate of duty will be 5% ad valorem.Duty rates are provided for your convenience and are subject to change.
