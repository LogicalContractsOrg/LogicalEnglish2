- rules.l4: a shortened selection of the examples of L4's foundation course
  (doc/courses/foundation/module-4-examples.l4: the benefit eligibility rule
  and the income tax with two of its three brackets), with a rule over a
  list, two records and six #EVAL lines added.
- sale.l4: the sale contract of L4's reference manual
  (doc/reference/regulative/deontic-example.l4), with shorter names and
  types, a fixed price, and eight #TRACE lines and one #EVAL line added.
- l4run.mjs: ts-apps/jl4-web/scripts/test-wasm-integration.mjs; its first
  lines say what was changed.

The two .l4 files carry no notice of their own, because the tests of the
translator name their lines. The other files of this folder are new.
