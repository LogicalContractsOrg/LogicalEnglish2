lomo-1.1.0/ is an unmodified copy of files of the LOMO repository,
https://code.europa.eu/drpm/lomo, branch `development` at commit cfe522d
(the same files as release v1.1.0, 21 October 2026): the data files (data/),
the ontology and its four word lists (ontologies/*.ttl), the validity rules
(shapes/), the search queries of its scripts/rdf-to-table/queries/ (queries/),
the list of changes, the README and the licence.
