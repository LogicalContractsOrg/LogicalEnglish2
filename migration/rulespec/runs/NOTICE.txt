Results of Axiom's engine on RuleSpec programs

rulespec_oracle.py writes the .json files of this folder. Each file is
about one program of rulespec-us, the RuleSpec corpus of the Axiom
Foundation:

    https://github.com/TheAxiomFoundation/rulespec-us
    commit f468c8daeb3c0e7184b85be7fb97585475d74a1b (4 October 2026)

A file holds the names of the program's test cases, the values the tests
expect and the values the engine gave, and, under "lowered", the formulas
of the program as the engine lowers them (axiom-rules-engine,
https://github.com/TheAxiomFoundation/axiom-rules-engine, commit
e9757b47c9983ac3a0873827e064b790e03c29a4; MIT licence; the engine is not
copied here).

The test names, the expected values and the formulas come from rulespec-us.
They are the Axiom Foundation's, under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International licence (CC BY 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode
They are here in a changed form: a program wrote them as JSON. The licence
has a disclaimer of warranties and a limitation of liability (its
Section 5). The attribution rulespec-us suggests is: "Axiom Foundation
RuleSpec corpus (CC BY 4.0), https://github.com/TheAxiomFoundation".

The Axiom Foundation did not make these files, and nothing here implies
that it endorses them.

gate.txt and roundtrip.txt are results of the translator's own checks
(gate.pl, roundtrip.pl).
