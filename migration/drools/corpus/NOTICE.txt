Two of the rule bases here, with their Java fact models, are examples of Drools. They come from the
`drools-examples` module of the Apache KIE (incubating) repository, https://github.com/apache/incubator-kie
(earlier named apache/incubator-kie-drools):
  drl/Fire.drl               drools-examples/src/main/resources/org/drools/examples/fire/simple/Fire.drl
  drl/HonestPolitician.drl   drools-examples/src/main/resources/org/drools/examples/honestpolitician/HonestPolitician.drl
  java/org/drools/examples/fire/Alarm.java, Fire.java, Room.java, Sprinkler.java
  java/org/drools/examples/honestpolitician/Hope.java, Politician.java
                             the same paths under drools-examples/src/main/java/
